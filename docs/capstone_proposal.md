## Project Duration
Mid May 2026 to November 2026

## Problem Statement
Schools generate different kinds of waste every day, but students often struggle to sort waste correctly into recyclable, compost, and landfill categories. Incorrect segregation reduces recycling efficiency and increases environmental impact.

## Aim
To build an AI-powered waste segregation assistant that can identify waste items from images and guide students to dispose of them correctly.

## Objectives
- Classify waste into recyclable, compost, and landfill categories
- Use computer vision to make predictions from camera images
- Build a simple web app for real-time use
- Show progress through a visual dashboard
- Create awareness through AI-generated educational tips

## Data Requirements
- Images of recyclable waste
- Images of compostable waste
- Images of landfill waste
- Balanced dataset collected from school and daily life objects

## Expected Outcome
A working AI system that can classify waste items and help students separate waste correctly, supported by a dashboard and project documentation.

## Significance
This project promotes environmental awareness, practical AI use, and better waste management in schools.
